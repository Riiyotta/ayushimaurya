Your virtual wardrobe

I designed a digital wardrobe that looks and works like a real one. You can store, organise and browse your clothes in 3D.

My role

Duration

01The idea

Designed like a real wardrobe

Most wardrobe apps show your clothes as a list of photos. I wanted to put them in a space that feels familiar, with doors that open, and rails and shelves to browse.

I built it as an experimental AI-powered prototype. The goal was to turn an idea into something you can actually click through and try.
