VISUAL DESIGN · CLINIC REFERRALS

Give ₹500,
Get ₹500.

I designed a referral and gift card feature for a clinic app. Your friends get ₹500 off their Annual Health Checkup, and you get a ₹500 gift card for every booking they make.

Type
Visual & interaction
Product
Clinic app
Platform
Mobile app

The goal

Get more people to book an Annual Health Checkup at the clinic through referrals from existing patients.

The challenge

Referral screens often look like coupons. I wanted this one to feel like a real gift, while staying clear about what you get, when you get it and how to use it.

What I designed

The referral page, the screen you see when you unlock a gift card, and a wallet for the gift cards you've earned. I also set the visual style for all three.

The outcome

An increase in clinic orders and in app downloads.

The three screens

One screen each to share, unlock and redeem a gift card. Tap any screen to see it full size.
