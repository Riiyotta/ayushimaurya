Chapter02

The unexpected plot twist

In class 12, I chose Science. It was a hard time in my life, and I wasn't sure which direction to take.

I looked into fashion design at NIFT, and I also cleared NATA, the architecture entrance exam. In the end, I dropped both and started with Communication Design.

From there I moved to UI/UX, and then to product design.

it wasn't a straight path ✿
